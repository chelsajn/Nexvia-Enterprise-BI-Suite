# User Guide: Enterprise BI Suite

## Who sees what
Each region manager sees only their own region's sales data. Roles: North America, Europe, Pacific.

## Pages
- **Executive Summary:** headline KPIs (Total Sales, Margin %, YoY growth, Rejection Rate, Headcount), monthly sales trend and sales by region.
- **Sales Detail:** sales by category and subcategory, top 10 products and a margin matrix.
- **Operations:** top vendors by purchase value, vendor rejection rates and headcount by department.

## How to use it
- Use the Year and Region slicers on the Executive page to filter the report.
- To see details for a region, right-click its bar in "Total Sales by Region", choose Drill through, then Sales Detail. Use the back arrow to return.
- Expand a category in the Sales Detail matrix with the plus icon to see subcategories.

## Notes
- Data refreshes nightly at 2 AM from the SQL Server warehouse.
- Headcount and purchasing figures are not filtered by region.
